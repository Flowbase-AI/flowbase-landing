What we make

Contract manufacturing for engineers who need parts that match the print — tolerances held, surfaces finished, paperwork included.

  • CNC milling

    3- and 5-axis work on aluminum, steel, stainless, and engineering plastics. Tight pockets, complex contours, fixture-ready setups.

  • Turning

    Shafts, bushings, threaded fittings, and live-tooling features. Small diameters through large blanks.

  • Sheet metal

    Laser cut, brake-formed, and welded assemblies. Enclosures, brackets, panels, and frames.

  • Finishing

    Anodize, powder coat, passivate, bead blast, and assembly. Ready for install, not just inspection.
